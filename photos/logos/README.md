# Client logos

Drop a logo here named after the client's slug in `data.py` (`CLIENTS`), for example `safaricom.svg` or `kcb.png`, and rebuild. The client strip on the home page then shows the logo instead of the name. SVG is best; PNG should have a transparent or white background and be at least 300 px wide.

`unilever.svg` is from Simple Icons (CC0).
