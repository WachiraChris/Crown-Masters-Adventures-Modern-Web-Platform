Corporate fitness and wellness programmes

Crown Masters Adventures runs corporate fitness programmes at offices in Nairobi and beyond. Our trainers fitness-test every participant, design sessions for your team and lead them on site. All you need is an open space, such as a boardroom, parking area, rooftop or nearby field.

Aerobics

Circuit training

Why companies bring fitness to the office

Most office staff sit for most of the day, and many cannot fit a gym in around work and Nairobi traffic. Over time that builds an inactive workforce and raises the risk of lifestyle diseases.

  • No commute to a gym: sessions happen at work
  • Colleagues train together and keep each other going
  • A fitness test for every participant
  • Nutrition guidance alongside the workouts
  • Programmes designed for your team's fitness levels
  • Sessions can be scheduled around shifts

How a programme runs

  1. Planning call

    Team size, location, preferred days and any health considerations.

  2. Fitness tests

    A short test for each participant to set a starting point.

  3. Your programme

    Session types and frequency agreed with you.

  4. Sessions on site

    Our trainers lead every session at your premises.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions HR and admin teams ask us most.

What is corporate fitness?

Corporate fitness is a workout programme delivered at your workplace. Our trainers come to your premises, design sessions for your team and lead them, so staff exercise together without travelling to a gym after work.

What workouts do you offer?

Zumba, aerobics, calisthenics, high-intensity (insanity-style) workouts, interval training and circuit training, chosen to suit your team's fitness levels.

What space do we need?

An open area: a boardroom with the tables moved back, a parking space, a rooftop or a field near your office.

Is it suitable for staff who don't exercise?

Yes. Everyone does a fitness test first, and sessions are scaled so beginners and regular exercisers can train side by side. We also give nutrition guidance.

How often are sessions held?

Frequency is agreed with you; weekly and twice-weekly programmes are the most common choices for office teams.
