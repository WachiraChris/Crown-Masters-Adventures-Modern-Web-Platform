Corporate team building in Kenya

Crown Masters Adventures plans and runs corporate team building events in Kenya. We agree your goals, book a venue that fits your budget from 123 options, arrange transport and meals, and facilitate indoor, outdoor or adventure activities on the day, for teams from a handful of people to whole companies.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions HR and admin teams ask us most.

What is corporate team building?

Corporate team building is a planned day or retreat of group activities that helps colleagues communicate, cooperate and trust each other. A facilitator runs the activities and links them back to how the team works day to day.

How long does a team building event last?

Most events are a single day at a venue near Nairobi, roughly from a morning departure to an early evening return. Retreats in Naivasha, Nanyuki or the coast usually run two or three days with overnight stays.

Can you tailor activities to our goals?

Yes. Tell us what you want the team to come away with, such as better communication, a new team getting to know each other, or a reward for a strong year, and we design the programme around it.

Do you provide transport?

Yes. We arrange executive transport so your group travels to and from the venue together.

What should staff wear and bring?

Comfortable clothes and closed shoes for outdoor activities, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle. For overnight trips we send a packing list with the programme.

Planning a team event?

Send your group size, dates and budget. We reply with venue options and a quote.
