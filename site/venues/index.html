Choosing a venue

Straight answers to the questions HR and admin teams ask us most.

Which are the best team building venues near Nairobi?

For a one-day event, common choices within about two hours of Nairobi include Lukenya Getaway, Maanzoni Lodge, Brackenhurst Hotel, Fred's Ranch in Kitengela, and gardens such as Stedmark and Zen Gardens. The best one depends on group size, budget and the activities you want.

Where should we go for a two-day team building retreat?

Naivasha, Elementaita and Nanyuki are the usual choices for overnight retreats. They are a few hours from Nairobi and have resorts with space for group activities.

Which venues are best for adventure activities?

Sagana, at venues such as Rapids Camp, Savage Wilderness and Sagana Getaway, is known for white-water rafting and river activities.

Can you book a venue that is not on this list?

Yes. The list covers the venues we use most, and we can book others on request.
