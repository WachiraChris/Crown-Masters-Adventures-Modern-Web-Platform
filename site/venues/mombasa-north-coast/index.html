Team building in North Coast: questions

Straight answers to the questions HR and admin teams ask us most.

How far is North Coast from Nairobi?

Typical travel time is about 1 hour by air or 5 hours by SGR train. Road times vary with traffic, so we plan departures to arrive before the first activity.

Is Mombasa & North Coast better for a day trip or an overnight stay?

It is best for overnight stays; the travel time makes a same-day return tiring.

Which team building venues do you use in Mombasa & North Coast?

We work with 11 venues here, including Baobab Beach Resort, North Coast Beach Hotel, The Reef Hotel, Voyager Beach Resort, Bamburi Beach Resort. See the full list on this page and add any of them to your quote.
