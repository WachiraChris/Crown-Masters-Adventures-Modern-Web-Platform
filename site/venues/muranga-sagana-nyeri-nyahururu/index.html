Crown Masters Adventures books and runs team building events at 14 venues in Murang'a, Sagana, Nyeri & Nyahururu, about 1.5 to 3 hours by road from Nairobi. Team building in Central and Sagana: questions

Straight answers to the questions HR and admin teams ask us most.

How far is Central and Sagana from Nairobi?

Typical travel time is about 1.5 to 3 hours by road. Road times vary with traffic, so we plan departures to arrive before the first activity.

Is Murang'a, Sagana, Nyeri & Nyahururu better for a day trip or an overnight stay?

It works for both. Closer venues suit day trips, and the resorts suit one or two nights.

Which team building venues do you use in Murang'a, Sagana, Nyeri & Nyahururu?

We work with 14 venues here, including Nokras Murang'a, Nokras Riverine, Nokras Enkare, Golden Palm, Mima Gardens. See the full list on this page and add any of them to your quote.
